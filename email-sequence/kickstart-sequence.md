# MailPoet setup (≈20 minutes)

1. **Upload the PDF**: *Media → Add New* → upload `zmafit-7-day-fat-loss-kickstart.pdf` → copy its URL into `[PDF_LINK]` in Email 1.
2. **Create a list**: *MailPoet → Lists → New List* → name it `Kickstart Guide`.
3. **Create the form**: *MailPoet → Forms → New Form* → fields: **First name** (optional) + **Email** → "Subscribe to list": `Kickstart Guide` → button text: **Send me the guide** → success message: *"Check your inbox — your guide is on its way."*
4. **Landing page**: create a page at `zmafit.com/free-guide` with a headline, the book cover image (`lead-magnet/out/pages/page-01.png`), 4 bullets of what's inside and the MailPoet form. Use this URL in the Meta ad.
5. **Automation**: *MailPoet → Automations → New automation → "Welcome new subscribers"* (or build from scratch):
   - Trigger: **Someone subscribes** → list `Kickstart Guide`
   - Send Email 1 → **Delay 1 day** → Email 2 → **Delay 2 days** → Email 3 → **Delay 2 days** → Email 4 → **Delay 2 days** → Email 5
6. **Sender**: *MailPoet → Settings → Basics* → "From" name: `[NAME] from ZmaFit`, reply-to: an inbox you read.
7. **Test**: subscribe with your own email and check that Email 1 arrives with a working PDF link (also check the spam folder).

> If your MailPoet version only allows a single welcome email in Automations, send Emails 2–5 as
> **Post notifications / scheduled newsletters** to the `Kickstart Guide` list, or upgrade MailPoet —
> check *MailPoet → Automations* to see which steps your plan includes.
