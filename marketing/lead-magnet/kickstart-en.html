How this plan works

Most people quit because the plan is too big. This one is built to be small enough to start today and steady enough to show results in 30 days.

Each weekWhat you doTime
3 workoutsWorkout A, B and C below, on non-consecutive days (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri)15–25 min
Daily walkBuild up to 7,000–9,000 steps a day20–40 min
Food habitsAdd one new habit each week (page 4)0 extra min
Check-inWeigh in and measure your waist once a week, same day, same time2 min
Realistic expectations: a healthy pace is roughly 0.5–1% of your body weight per week. Your waist measurement and how your clothes fit matter as much as the scale.

Progression

  • Week 1: 2 rounds of each workout. Learn the moves.
  • Week 2: 3 rounds.
  • Week 3: 3 rounds, add 2 reps or 5 seconds to every exercise.
  • Week 4: 4 rounds, or use the harder variation.

Before you start

Warm up for 3 minutes every time: marching on the spot, arm circles, 10 slow bodyweight squats, 10 hip hinges. If you have an injury, a heart condition, are pregnant or have any medical condition, check with your doctor first.

5 food habits (one per week)

No strict diet. Add one habit at a time and keep the earlier ones.

WeekHabitWhy it works
1Protein at every meal: eggs, chicken, fish, yoghurt, lentils, beansKeeps you full and protects muscle while you lose fat
2Half the plate vegetables or salad at lunch and dinnerMore food volume, fewer calories
3Swap sugary drinks for water, tea or coffee without sugarOne of the easiest ways to remove hundreds of calories a day
4Plan your snacks: fruit, nuts (a small handful), yoghurtStops random late-night snacking
+Sleep 7–8 hoursPoor sleep increases hunger and cravings

Simple plate guide

  • ½ plate: vegetables or salad
  • ¼ plate: protein, about the size of your palm
  • ¼ plate: carbs such as rice, bread, potatoes or pasta, about the size of your fist
  • A thumb of fats: olive oil, butter, nuts
You do not have to give up your favourite foods. Fat loss comes from your average over the week, not from one meal. Keep the foods you love, in portions that fit.

Protein ideas on a budget

Eggs · canned tuna or sardines · lentils and chickpeas · chicken thighs · plain yoghurt · cottage cheese · milk

What's next?

This kickstart is the same for everyone. It is a great start, but your body, schedule and equipment are not the same as everyone else's.

Stuck after week 2?

  • Check your portions for one week: most stalls come from snacks and drinks you don't count.
  • Add 1,500 steps a day.
  • Make the workouts harder (page 2), not longer.
  • Protect your sleep.
